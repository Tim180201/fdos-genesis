# FDOS Observation Model

Status: Conceptual Model
Release: Research Evolution R2.1

## Purpose

The Observation Model defines the organizational entry point of learning.

Observations describe organizational reality before validation.

Evidence validates organizational relevance.

## Definition

An Observation is a structured description of a potentially relevant organizational event, decision, outcome or condition.

An Observation is not automatically Evidence.

## Observation Sources

Observations may originate from:

- Project ADO
- Commits
- Pull Requests
- Architecture Decisions
- Test Results
- Releases
- Operational Incidents
- Customer Feedback
- Security Events
- Production Operations

## Observation Lifecycle

Reality
↓
Observation
↓
Evaluation
↓
Evidence (if validated)

Observations that do not demonstrate organizational relevance may be discarded without affecting the organizational knowledge base.

## Principles

- Observations describe organizational reality.
- Evidence validates organizational relevance.
- Observations remain traceable to their originating context.
- Observations never modify organizational standards directly.

## Compatibility

The Observation Model introduces no new FDOS Core object.

It provides a conceptual entry point for future learning capabilities while remaining fully compatible with the existing Evidence and Knowledge lifecycle.
