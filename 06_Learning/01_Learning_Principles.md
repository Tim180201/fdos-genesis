# FDOS Learning Principles

Status: Foundation Principle Set
Release: Learning Foundation R1.1

## Purpose

These principles define the immutable foundation of organizational learning within FDOS. All future learning capabilities, automation and AI support shall conform to these principles.

## Principles

### 1. Reality Before Interpretation
Reality exists independently of our interpretation.

### 2. Observation Before Evidence
Not every observation is evidence. Observations require evaluation before becoming evidence.

### 3. Evidence Before Knowledge
Organizational knowledge must originate from validated evidence.

### 4. Validation Before Standardization
No learning outcome becomes an organizational standard without validation and governance.

### 5. Traceability by Default
Every learning artifact shall remain traceable to its originating context.

### 6. Human Governance Remains Final
AI may recommend. Humans decide.

### 7. Learning Must Improve Future Decisions
Learning exists to improve future organizational decisions rather than document the past.

### 8. Organizational Learning Over Individual Memory
Knowledge should become organizational assets instead of remaining with individuals.

### 9. Stable Core, Adaptive Learning
Learning may evolve continuously; the FDOS Core evolves only through established governance.
