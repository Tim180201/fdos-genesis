# Organizational Learning Lifecycle

Status: Lifecycle Model
Release: Research Evolution R2.2

## Purpose

This document defines the continuous lifecycle of organizational learning within FDOS.

Learning is not a linear process. It is a continuous feedback loop between organizational reality and organizational improvement.

## Lifecycle

Reality
↓
Observation
↓
Evidence
↓
Knowledge Candidate
↓
Validated Knowledge
↓
Pattern Candidate
↓
Validated Pattern
↓
Proposal
↓
Capability
↓
FDOS Core
↓
Project Reuse
↓
New Reality

## Principles

- Learning is continuous.
- Organizational knowledge exists to improve future reality.
- Every reuse of organizational knowledge creates new learning opportunities.
- The lifecycle is governed through evidence and human review.

## Feedback Loop

The completion of one learning cycle becomes the starting point of the next. Organizational improvement therefore emerges through repeated application, observation and validation rather than isolated architectural decisions.
