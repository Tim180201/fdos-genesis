# FDOS Reality Model

Status: Foundation Model
Release: Learning Foundation R1.2

## Purpose

The Reality Model defines the conceptual starting point of organizational learning.

Learning does not begin with evidence.

Learning begins with reality.

## Learning Flow

Reality
↓
Observation
↓
Evidence
↓
Knowledge Candidate
↓
Validated Knowledge
↓

↓

↓

FDOS Core

## Definitions

### Reality
Reality is everything that actually happens within or around an organization, regardless of whether it is observed or documented.

### Observation
An observation is a conscious recognition of a relevant aspect of reality. Observations may be incomplete or incorrect and require evaluation.

### Evidence
Evidence is an observation that has been validated, contextualized and made traceable.

### Knowledge
Knowledge is organizational understanding derived from validated evidence.

## Core Principles

- Reality exists independently of observation.
- Not every reality becomes an observation.
- Not every observation becomes evidence.
- Not every evidence item becomes organizational knowledge.
- Organizational change always follows validated knowledge and governance.

## Compatibility

This model extends the conceptual foundation of FDOS without modifying the existing Evidence or Knowledge lifecycle. Existing projects and Project ADO remain fully compatible.
