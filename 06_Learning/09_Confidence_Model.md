# FDOS Confidence Model

Status: Confidence Framework
Release: Research Evolution R2.5

## Purpose

The Confidence Model defines how organizational learning artifacts are assessed based on the strength of supporting evidence.

Confidence represents trust in knowledge—not certainty.

## Applicable Artifacts

- Observation
- Evidence
- Knowledge
- Pattern
- Proposal

## Confidence Factors

Confidence may be influenced by:

- Number of independent projects
- Quality of evidence
- Successful reuse
- Production validation
- Human review
- Regression history
- Age and freshness

## Confidence Levels

- Hypothesis
- Emerging Knowledge
- Validated Knowledge
- Organizational Standard

## Principles

- Confidence is evidence-driven.
- Confidence evolves over time.
- Confidence never replaces governance.
- Low confidence is not failure; it indicates the need for further validation.
