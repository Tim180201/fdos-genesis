# AI Collaboration Evolution

Status: Evolution Charter
Release: Research Evolution R2.0

## Purpose

This document defines the long-term evolution of AI collaboration within FDOS.

The evolution is based on organizational responsibilities rather than individual agent roles.

## Organizational Responsibilities

### Development Agent
Mission: Build products and maintain project truth.

### Research Agent
Mission: Curate organizational knowledge by evaluating structured organizational observations and preparing evidence-based proposals.

### Learning System
Mission: Structure organizational observations into a reusable learning process.

The Learning System is an organizational capability, not an autonomous decision maker.

### Human Governance
Mission: Review proposals and determine organizational truth.

## Collaboration Principle

AI agents do not compete for responsibility.

They cooperate through clearly defined organizational responsibilities.

## Evolution Rule

AI collaboration may evolve continuously.

Organizational governance and the FDOS Core evolve only through validated evidence and human review.
