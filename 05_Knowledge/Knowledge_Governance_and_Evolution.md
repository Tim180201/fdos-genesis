# Knowledge Governance and Evolution

Status: Governance Extension
Release: Research Evolution R2.3

## Purpose

This document defines how organizational knowledge is governed, maintained and evolved within FDOS.

Knowledge is a living organizational asset rather than a permanent truth.

## Principles

- Knowledge originates from validated evidence.
- Knowledge remains subject to continuous validation.
- Knowledge may evolve as organizational reality evolves.
- Knowledge may be superseded, deprecated or archived.
- Organizational learning continuously improves the knowledge base.

## Governance

Human Governance determines when organizational knowledge becomes authoritative, requires revision or is retired.

## Continuous Evolution

Reality continuously challenges organizational knowledge.

Learning continuously evaluates organizational reality.

Knowledge therefore evolves through repeated observation, evidence and governance.

## Guiding Statement

Knowledge is not permanent.

It is continuously challenged by reality.
