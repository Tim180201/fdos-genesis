# FDOS Architecture Map

Status: Canonical Architecture Overview  
Release: Architecture Consolidation R2.8

## Purpose

This document provides the high-level architecture of FDOS and describes how its major organizational capabilities interact.

It is the primary orientation document for understanding how Projects, Learning, Evidence, Knowledge, Governance, AI and the FDOS Core form one coherent system.

## Organizational Flow

```text
Projects
    ↓
Reality
    ↓
Learning
    ↓
Evidence
    ↓
Knowledge
    ↓
Patterns
    ↓
Capabilities
    ↓
FDOS Core
    ↓
Projects
```

## Cross-Cutting Capabilities

- AI supports execution and learning.
- Governance validates organizational evolution.
- Specifications define shared architectural standards.

## Architecture Principles

- Projects create organizational reality.
- Learning transforms reality into organizational understanding.
- Evidence validates organizational relevance.
- Knowledge captures validated organizational understanding.
- Governance determines organizational truth.
- The FDOS Core captures validated organizational capabilities.
- Organizational improvement is continuous and cyclical.
