# FDOS Charter Addendum v3.8 LTS

Status: Released  
Version: 3.8 LTS

## Added Charter Principle

FDOS exists to serve products, customers and organizations — never itself.

## Meaning

FDOS must never become an end in itself.

Every rule, capability, workflow, governance mechanism or specification must ultimately support real product development and organizational learning.

## Final Test

Every future FDOS change must answer:

Does this improve the development of real products?

If the answer is not clearly yes, the change must not enter the FDOS Core.

## Consequence

Documentation without operational value is rejected.

Governance without practical benefit is rejected.

Architecture without product impact is rejected.
