# ADR-0037 Architecture Admission Policy

Status: Accepted  
Version: 3.6

## Decision

FDOS adopts an Architecture Admission Policy instead of a hard architecture freeze.

## Context

The architecture should remain stable after v3.x, but must still evolve when reality proves the need.

## Consequence

New major concepts require evidence, review and ADR approval.

## Rationale

Stability is protected without becoming dogmatic.
