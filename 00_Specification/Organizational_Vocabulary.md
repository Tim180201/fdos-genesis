# FDOS Organizational Vocabulary

Status: Canonical Vocabulary
Release: Research Evolution R2.4

## Purpose

This document defines the canonical organizational language used throughout FDOS.

All concepts should use these definitions consistently.

## Core Terms

### Reality
Everything that actually happens within or around the organization.

### Observation
A structured recognition of relevant organizational reality.

### Evidence
A validated and traceable observation.

### Knowledge
Organizational understanding derived from validated evidence.

### Pattern
A reusable organizational abstraction validated across multiple contexts.

### Proposal
A suggested organizational improvement derived from validated knowledge or patterns.

### Capability
A standardized organizational ability adopted into FDOS.

### Governance
The process through which organizational truth and standards are determined.

### Learning
The continuous transformation of organizational reality into improved organizational decisions.

## Rule

All future FDOS specifications, AI constitutions and project documentation should use this vocabulary consistently.
